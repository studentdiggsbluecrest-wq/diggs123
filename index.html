About Me

I am Boakai Diggs, a student of Professional Diploma in Information Technology (PDIT), specializing in Software Engineering at BlueCrest University College, Liberia. I was born in Lofa-Bridge, Grand Cape Mount County, Liberia.

I am interested in software development, database systems, web design and using technology to solve everyday problems. I founded Tech For Tomorrow to help create practical technology solutions, particularly for education and communities.
